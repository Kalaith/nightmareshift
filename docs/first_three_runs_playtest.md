# First-three-runs playtest

Use this form with a player who has not played Nightmare Shift before. Do not
coach route, guideline, or cab-action decisions. Record one row immediately
after each of the player's first three attempts in
`docs/verification/first_three_runs_playtest.csv`.

## Ask after each attempt

1. What ended the attempt, in your own words?
2. Before the final decision, did you predict that this could end the attempt?
3. After seeing the result, do you understand why it happened?
4. Do you think a different choice could have avoided it?
5. What information, if any, did you need but could not find?
6. Which route tradeoff was hardest to explain?
7. Did a cab upgrade or action visibly help? What changed?

Use `yes`, `partly`, or `no` for the three comprehension ratings. Keep the
player's wording in the notes; do not translate uncertainty into a yes.

## Evidence gate

The early-run tuning gate requires five first-time players completing all three
attempts (15 rows). Human results are intentionally not generated by the bot.
