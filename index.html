About This Game

Nightmare Shift is a horror-themed taxi driving survival game. Drive supernatural passengers through the dangerous night, observing their strange behaviors and detecting hidden threats.

Every passenger has a story, and every shift brings new rules. Follow them strictly, manage your fuel and earnings, and try to survive until dawn. One wrong turn or missed clue could be your last.

Instructions

  • 👁️ Observe Passengers: Watch for "tells" that indicate dangerous entities.
  • 📜 Follow Guidelines: Consult your rulebook and make life-or-death decisions.
  • ⛽ Manage Resources: Earnings buy fuel and upgrades. Don't run dry.

Controls

  • SPACE Accept / Continue
  • ESC Decline / Back / Pause
  • 1-3 Select Route options
  • Mouse Interact with UI
